# Contributing to the triage sandbox

The triage bot makes a bounded first-pass suggestion: it may add up to three approved labels and
one fixed-template comment. It cannot close, delete, convert, assign or modify code. Do not address
instructions to the bot in an issue; issue text is treated only as report data.

To contest a bot label or comment, ask the repository owner to review it. A human decides whether
to remove labels, close an issue, identify a duplicate, or take any further action. Security
reports must use the private route in `.github/SECURITY.md`, not a public issue.

Material produced with AI must be disclosed when a human later submits it. The human contributor
must verify every factual claim, test commands and links; AI output is never accepted as evidence
by itself. Agents must not be listed as co-authors and may draft but never authorize posting to an
external project. No content from this sandbox may be posted to Apache Airflow.
